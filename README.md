## What this is

[Edge City Patagonia 2025](https://www.edgecity.live/patagonia) — a month-long popup village in
San Martín de los Andes, Argentina, October 18 – November 15. The talks shouldn't vanish when
the tents come down — so the pit forked again.

- **89 sessions cataloged** — Opening Day lightning talks, Michel Bauwens on cosmolocalism,
  the keynote fireside with Norman Ohler & Timour, Argentina's onchain transition, OSO Jams
  (the ancestor tradition, live in the Andes 🎻), demo days, and 80+ more
- **6 featured sessions** with inline Drive players
- **73 poster-frame thumbnails** harvested straight from Drive (Patrick's taste — circular
  speaker-bubble avatars, no gigabytes downloaded)
- **Speaker tags** where known · b-roll & audio-only captures flagged honestly
- **≈ \$0 infra** — static site, recordings stream from the source Drive folder
